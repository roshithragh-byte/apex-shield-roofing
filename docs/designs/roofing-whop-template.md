## Problem Statement
US roofing contractors operate in a high-ticket, low-trust environment ($8k-$30k average replacements, $500-$2,500 emergency repairs). Today, contractors suffer from ghosted quotes, long sales cycles, and a 10-15 hour weekly drag chasing uncollected net-30 balances and physical checks. Homeowners abandon generic contact forms due to friction, lack of transparent pricing, and absent point-of-sale financing.

## Demand Evidence
Roofing contractors lose thousands each month to ghosted bids and slow check payments. When payroll ($40k+) is due on Friday, having $80k+ in completed job balances stuck in uncollected checks creates existential anxiety. Securing upfront commitments ($149 inspections, $500 project deposits) paired with an automated card-on-file Setup Intent resolves their cash-flow leakage at the source.

## Status Quo
Paper estimates and contracts signed on clipboards, followed by manual paper checks picked up on job sites or delayed net-30 QuickBooks invoices. Generic WordPress/Wix sites with Contact Form 7 have a 40%+ lead dropoff rate because they lack instant booking, upfront pricing transparency, or consumer financing (Klarna, Splitit, Afterpay).

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
- **Target User:** Owner-operator or General Manager of a 5-20 person local roofing enterprise ($1M–$5M/yr annual revenue).
- **Narrowest Wedge:** A turnkey, conversion-optimized website template deployed on Whop Websites that accepts instant online bookings for a $149 drone/hazard roof inspection or a $500 project deposit, capturing card-on-file via Whop Setup Intents, and empowering the contractor to auto-bill or dispatch the final balance via Whop Invoices API.

## Constraints
1. **Whop Websites Native:** Must be scaffolded and deployed to a live `whop.site` / `whop.app` address using Whop CLI (`@whop/cli`) or Whop AI.
2. **Whop Payments Exclusivity:** Zero Stripe, PayPal, or custom card forms. Must use official Whop Payment Elements: `PaymentElement` (or `CheckoutElement`), `ExpressCheckoutElement` (Apple Pay / Google Pay), and mandatory `BrandingElement`.
3. **Leads API:** "Request an Estimate" form must create a real Lead via the Whop REST API.
4. **Card-on-File & Invoicing:** Capture payment method on file via Setup Intent during deposit checkout, then demonstrate the final balance billing via Whop Invoices API (auto-charge or manual send).
5. **Analytics & Pixel Tracking:** Fire at least 3 custom pixel events with `whop.track`: `estimate_requested`, `service_viewed`, and `deposit_started`.
6. **Reusable Template Architecture:** A single central config (`roofing.config.ts`) allows any roofing contractor to swap company name, logo, phone, license #, service areas/zip codes, and pricing plans without touching layout or component code.

## Premises
1. Small online commitments ($149 inspection / $500 deposit) convert homeowners immediately if high-trust signals and BNPL financing options are present.
2. Card-on-file via Setup Intent at deposit stage eliminates post-job payment chasing.
3. The template must be completely customizable via a single config schema without code edits.

## Approaches Considered
### Approach A: High-Converting Agency Template (Recommended)
- Config-driven site (`roofing.config.ts`), 3 Whop plans (Inspection $149, Deposit $500, Flat-rate Leak Repair $2,499), Whop Payment + Express Checkout + Branding Elements, Leads API integration, `whop.track` pixel events, and an interactive Roofer Invoicing simulator demonstrating the final balance collection cycle.
- Effort: M (CC: ~35 min). Risk: Low. Reuses Whop Elements, Whop SDK, and standard Cloudflare Workers/Vite runtime.

### Approach B: Lean Single-Page Pitch Site
- Minimal static site with embedded Whop checkout links and simple lead form.
- Effort: S. Risk: Low. Rejected because it fails to vividly demonstrate the card-on-file to final invoice lifecycle, weakening the pitch to contractors.

### Approach C: Visual Roof-Scope Quote Engine
- Adds satellite roof area and pitch estimation calculator before checkout.
- Effort: L. Risk: Med-High. Rejected as premature complexity that distracts from core Whop Payments and Invoicing workflows.

## Recommended Approach
Approach A: The High-Converting Agency Template. It delivers the complete end-to-end commercial story required to pitch roofing contractors, nails every technical requirement in the Whop Websites and Whop Elements specification, and provides an out-of-the-box template that an agency can replicate for dozens of contractors in minutes.
